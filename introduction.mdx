## What you can build

XPayLabs provides the building blocks for a wide range of payment use cases:

- **E-commerce checkouts** — Accept cards, digital wallets, and local payment methods
- **Marketplace payouts** — Send funds to sellers, vendors, and contractors globally
- **Subscription billing** — Charge customers on a recurring schedule
- **In-person payments** — Process face-to-face transactions with our Terminal SDK

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [dashboard.xpaylabs.com](https://dashboard.xpaylabs.com) and complete identity verification to unlock live payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your API keys">
    From the Dashboard, navigate to **Developers → API Keys** to copy your secret key. Use your test key during development — no real money moves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a payment">
    Send a `POST /payments` request with the amount, currency, and payment method. XPayLabs handles authorization, capture, and settlement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listen for events">
    Register a webhook endpoint to receive real-time notifications when payments succeed, fail, or are refunded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| **Payment** | A single charge against a payment method |
| **Settlement** | The transfer of funds from XPayLabs to your bank account |
| **Refund** | A full or partial return of a captured payment |
| **Payout** | A funds transfer from your XPayLabs balance to a third party |
| **Webhook** | An HTTP callback triggered by a payment event |

<Tip>
  Use your **test API key** during development. Test mode is completely isolated from live transactions — you can create payments, trigger failures, and test webhooks without any real money moving.
</Tip>
